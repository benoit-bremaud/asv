## Findings to act on

1. 🔴 Must — UI-01 sub-check, pre-existing (same on `origin/main` a2154d0), not caused by #64: at 390 × 844 the "confidentialité" legal page overflows the viewport and cannot scroll, so its last rows and "fermer" are out of reach, from the entry footer and from the account panel (→ #126). Likely cause: the user-agent `dialog { height: fit-content }` wins over `inset: var(--gap)` in `.legal-page`. Suggested fix (not applied, after the `web-ui` study):

   ```diff
   -.legal-page { position: fixed; inset: var(--gap); z-index: 30; box-sizing: border-box; width: auto; max-width: …
   +.legal-page { position: fixed; inset: var(--gap); z-index: 30; box-sizing: border-box; width: auto; height: auto; max-height: calc(100dvh - 2 * var(--gap)); max-width: …
   ```

   The owner may waive it for PR #125 like #121 and #124, since the diff does not touch it.

Seen and not filed (already tracked): `favicon.ico` 404 (#62); the "Si l'adresse peut recevoir un code…" banner stays after sign-in and pushes the header 45 px down (#68); legal pages and search overlay drawn over the screen without a backdrop (#111); document title "asv" on every screen (#124, waived); no `aria-invalid` (#121, waived).
