# Personas — asv

Personas are archetypes built from the Caramail testimonies in `docs/research/caramail.md`.
They are design tools, not real people.

## Nathalie, 44 — the nostalgic regular

- Spent her student nights on Caramail in 2001, met friends in the "Poésie" room.
- Today: busy job, phone in hand in the evening, tired of Instagram.
- Wants: find the old atmosphere, chat lightly, maybe meet people from her city.
- Frustrations: sign-up forms, photos, being harassed in private messages.
- asv must: let her in in seconds, show her city, make ignore obvious.

## Karim, 38 — the joker

- Remembers the pranks and smileys of the old chat rooms.
- Wants: banter in a busy room, smileys, creating a room with friends for the evening.
- Frustrations: dead rooms, heavy moderation that blocks every word.
- asv must: make rooms feel alive, keep smileys, moderate fairly and transparently.

## Léa, 27 — the curious newcomer

- Never knew Caramail, heard about it in a nostalgia thread.
- Wants: try something different from dating apps, talk without being judged on a photo.
- Frustrations: not understanding the codes ("ASV ?"), feeling watched.
- asv must: explain the codes playfully, let her choose "not specified" for gender.

## Marc, 51 — the volunteer moderator

- Was a "modo" in a 2000s forum, trusted by the owner.
- Wants: handle reports quickly from his phone, expel abusers without debate.
- Frustrations: complex back-offices, passwords to remember.
- asv must: give a clear report list and a passkey sign-in.

## Benoît — the owner

- Runs asv alone, on free hosting.
- Wants: no surprise cost, legal peace of mind, a codebase that stays simple.
- asv must: fit the Cloudflare free plan, document legal duties, invite moderators safely.
